Gmail Cleaner

Move all mail from senders you choose to Trash. Everything runs on this computer.

1Connect your Gmail

Enter your Gmail address and a Google app password (not your normal password).

How do I get an app password?
  1. Turn on 2-Step Verification for your Google account, if it isn't already.
  2. Open App passwords, name it “Gmail Cleaner”, and click Create.
  3. Copy the 16-character code Google shows and paste it above.

Work and school accounts often block app passwords. Your password stays in this program’s memory only and is forgotten when you close it. You can revoke it any time on the App passwords page.
